Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

KMB Electric LLC ("KMB Electric", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through kmbelectric.com and how we use it.

What we collect

When you request an estimate through our website, we collect the details you type into the form: your name, phone number, the service you are asking about, and any message you include. If you call, text or email us, we keep the contact details and notes needed to quote and schedule your work.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request and provide an estimate.
  • To schedule, perform and follow up on electrical work you hire us for.
  • To keep records required for permits, inspections and warranties.

We do not sell your information. We do not add you to marketing lists or send promotional messages unless you ask us to.

Sharing

We share your information only with people and services needed to do the job: for example, the form service that delivers your website request to our inbox, building departments when we pull a permit for your project, and suppliers when materials must be shipped to your address.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand how visitors use it. We do not use advertising trackers.

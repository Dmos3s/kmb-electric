The Job

What a Level 2 install from KMB includes

A Level 2 charger runs on a dedicated 240 volt circuit, the same kind of circuit that feeds a dryer or a range. Done right it is a clean, permitted install that adds value to the house and charges most EVs overnight. Done wrong it trips breakers, overheats, or fails inspection when you sell. We do it right the first time.

At a glance

  • A look at your panel and a load calculation, so the charger fits the service you have
  • A dedicated 240 volt circuit, typically 40 to 60 amps depending on the charger
  • Hardwired or a NEMA 14-50 outlet, inside the garage or weatherproof outside
  • Any UL listed charger: Tesla Wall Connector, ChargePoint, Emporia, Wallbox, Grizzl-E and more
  • Permit pulled and inspection passed through the Pikes Peak Regional Building Department
  • Panel upgrade or load management options if your service is already near its limit

Questions

EV Charger Installation FAQ

Can my panel handle an EV charger?

Usually, yes. We do a load calculation on your existing service before quoting. Many 200 amp homes have room; a 100 amp service may need a smaller charger, a load management device or a panel upgrade. We tell you which before any work starts.

Hardwired or plug-in outlet?

Both are fine when installed correctly. A hardwired charger is cleaner and required for some higher amperage units. A NEMA 14-50 outlet gives you flexibility if you change vehicles. We walk you through the trade off for your setup.

Do I need a permit for an EV charger in Colorado Springs?

Yes. A new 240 volt circuit requires an electrical permit and inspection in Colorado Springs and El Paso County. We pull it and schedule the inspection as part of the job.

Can you install the charger outside?

Yes. Outdoor installs use weather rated equipment and conduit, and we place the charger so the cable reaches your parking spot without crossing a walkway.

How long does the install take?

Most garage installs are done in a single visit once the permit is issued. Longer runs, finished walls or a panel upgrade add time, and we tell you that in the estimate.

Which chargers do you install?

Any UL listed Level 2 charger, including Tesla Wall Connector, ChargePoint Home Flex, Emporia, Wallbox and Grizzl-E. If you already bought one, we install it. If not, we recommend one that fits your vehicle and panel.

Good To Know

Questions people ask before they call

Is KMB Electric licensed and insured?

Yes. KMB Electric LLC holds Colorado Electrical Contractor license #102799 and carries liability insurance. Every job is permitted and inspected where the code requires it.

Do you pull permits and schedule inspections?

Yes, on every job that needs one. We pull the permit through the Pikes Peak Regional Building Department, meet the inspector, and give you the paperwork so the work shows clean on your permit record when you sell.

What areas do you serve?

Colorado Springs and the Pikes Peak region, including Monument, Black Forest, Falcon, Peyton, Fountain, Security-Widefield, Manitou Springs, Woodland Park and Calhan.

Do you offer a military discount?

Yes. Active duty, Guard, Reserve and veterans get a discount on every job. KMB is veteran owned and operated, so just mention it when you call.

Do you do commercial work?

Residential work is our core, and we take on small scale commercial tenant finishes such as office and retail improvements and lighting updates. Large commercial projects are not a fit.

How do I get an estimate, and what does it cost?

Estimates are free. Call or text 719-963-5191, use the Text us button, or send the form on this page. A photo of your panel or the job site helps us quote it right. You get a written scope and a straight price, no sales call.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Weekends are closed. Texts and form requests sent after hours get a reply the next business morning.
